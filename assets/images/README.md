# Photos to Include

Drop your photos directly into this folder (`assets/images/`). Here are suggested photo types that make a dating portfolio pop:

1. **`hero.jpg` / `profile.jpg`**: A clear, smiling headshot or waist-up photo with good lighting (no sunglasses/hats).
2. **`activity_1.jpg` / `activity_2.jpg`**: Doing a hobby he loves (hiking, cooking, playing an instrument, sports, etc.).
3. **`social.jpg`**: A group shot or event photo showing he has fun with friends.
4. **`pet.jpg`**: With a pet or animal (instant charm points).
5. **`travel.jpg`**: An aesthetic travel or outdoor shot.
6. **`candid_or_funny.jpg`**: A goofy, candid, or meme-worthy photo that shows personality and a sense of humor.
